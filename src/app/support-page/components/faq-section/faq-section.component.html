AskSphere is a cutting-edge NLP-based Learner Support System designed to simplify the learning process. It allows students and teachers to create accounts, join or create classes, share announcements, upload course materials, and interact with AI models for querying and understanding course content.

To create a class on AskSphere, log in to your account, navigate to the "Create Class" section, and fill in the required details such as class name, subject, and description. Once created, you can invite students to join your class through unique invitation codes.

AskSphere supports a wide range of content types, including PDFs, Word documents, PowerPoint presentations, and images. These materials can be uploaded to specific classes and shared with students for easy access and reference.

The AI model in AskSphere leverages natural language processing (NLP) to help students understand complex concepts and answer their queries. By analyzing the uploaded course materials, the AI can provide accurate and contextually relevant answers, enhancing the learning experience.
